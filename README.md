My personal watchface design for a smartwatch running on Wear OS. The watch face is inspired by System Shock Remake. I made the watch face like trioptimum would have made it.

# Features
- Custom battery charge
- Custom heart rate
- Custom unread messages
- Custom steps
- For greater authenticity, Shodan appears when the heart rate is high.

# Installation Guide
## Step 1: Prepare your watch
On your watch, go to Settings -> About watch -> Software info.  
Tap Software version 7 times until you see a message saying "Developer mode turned on".  
Go back to the main Settings menu and open the new Developer options.  
Turn ON ADB debugging and Wireless debugging.  
Tap Wireless debugging and note down the IP address and Port.
## Step 2: Install via phone  
Download a free app like Bugjaeger or Geminiman Wear OS Manager from the Google Play on your Android phone.  
Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi network as your watch.  
Open the app, tap the Connect icon, and enter the IP address and Port from your watch.  
Once connected, go to the "Apps" tab, click the "+"  , and select my downloaded .apk file.
Wait for the installation to finish.
## Step 3: Activate the watch face
Long-press your current watch face, scroll to the end of the list, tap Add watch face, and select the System Shock Remake face.
